**ricercar 0.10.0** lets you browse your library by composer, helps you set up your audio output on first launch, highlights synced lyrics word by word and sorts your playlists.

## What's new

- **Composers.** A Composers page in the sidebar lists everyone found in the COMPOSER tag of your files. A composer's page shows their albums and their works, grouped by album, with Play, Shuffle and Add to queue. A track's menu has *Go to composer*, and search shows matching composers. UPnP control points also get a Composers folder.
- **Setup assistant.** On a fresh install, an assistant walks through the interface language, your music folders, plugins from the catalogue (installed after you confirm what is downloaded and from where, with their sign-in right there), then an output (hardware `hw:` outputs first, named by their card), what your DAC plays natively, and fixed volume. *Later* skips it at any step. Your music is scanned once you finish or skip it; until then it comes back at each start. It does not appear on upgrades; Settings → Setup assistant opens it again.
- **Word-by-word lyrics.** When synced lyrics time each word (enhanced LRC, `<mm:ss.xx>` inside a line), the current line lights up word by word. Line-by-line lyrics work as before; *Estimate word timing* in Settings → Appearance (off by default) lights up their words at an estimated pace.
- **Resizable sidebar.** Drag its right edge (180 to 480 px); double-click the edge for the default width. The width is kept between runs.
- **Sorted playlists.** A playlist's page, local or from a plugin, has a sort menu: the playlist's own order, *Recently added*, title, artist, album or length. The choice applies to every playlist and is kept between runs; Play, Shuffle and Add to queue follow it. The playlist itself is not reordered. Tracks added to local playlists from now on remember when; older ones count as added in their playlist order.
- **Fixes.**
  - Playlists and favourites changed on a plugin's service while ricercar runs (a playlist made on the service's website) now show up: plugin libraries are read again when the window gets the focus back (at most every 5 minutes), with the new refresh button next to *Playlists*, or when the plugin reports a change.
  - Long sign-in addresses no longer push the plugin sign-in dialog off screen.
  - A few interface strings (the search scopes, *System*) now show translated.

## For plugin authors

- `lyrics.get`: a synced line may carry an optional `words` list (`{"time_ms": …, "text": "…"}`). Older versions ignore it. See `docs/plugins.md`.
- Tracks listed by a playlist may carry `added_at` (Unix seconds, or an RFC 3339 date) for *Recently added*. Without it, the host takes the end of the playlist as the most recent. Older versions ignore it.
- New notification `library.changed` (plugin → host, no params): the user's library changed outside ricercar; the `library.*` lists are read again. Older versions ignore it.

> From 0.6.0 or later, *Update now* in Settings → About installs this version, checked against the signed `SHA256SUMS`.

> Plugins are written by third parties and are not reviewed by the ricercar project. They run with your permissions: install only plugins you trust, and check that they respect the terms of the service they use.

**From source:** `cargo build --release` with Rust 1.92 or newer (see the README for per-distro dependencies).
