**ricercar 0.9.0** plays DSD files and SACD images, adds plugins from anywhere, gives every album, playlist and artist page the same actions, and shows when a network stream is loading.

## What's new

- **DSD.** DSF and DFF files (DST-compressed DFF too), DSD64 and up, stereo.
  - By default they are converted to 24-bit PCM, which plays on any DAC. This path is not bit-perfect, and the signal path says so. The SACD reference level sits 6 dB below full scale, so DSD sounds quieter than most PCM masters.
  - **DoP and native DSD (experimental)** send the DSD bits untouched to a `hw:` DAC, at fixed volume. Pick them per output in Settings → Audio output. They have not been tested on a DSD-capable DAC yet; reports are welcome. A DAC without DSD support plays DoP as loud noise, so keep the volume down the first time.
- **SACD images.** An `.iso` SACD image in your library shows as its album, one track per track of its stereo area, plain or DST-compressed. Tracks play gapless, through the same DSD path.
- **Plugins from anywhere.** Besides the community hub, the Plugins page adds a plugin from a link to its program, a GitHub repository (the right binary of its latest release), another index, or a file. Nothing runs before you confirm what will be installed and from where.
- **The same actions everywhere.** Album, playlist (local or from a plugin) and artist pages share one bar: Play, Shuffle, Add to queue and a menu with Play next, Add to playlist, Start radio and the page's own entries. Queueing a plugin playlist or album takes all its tracks. Play and Shuffle on an artist known only through a plugin now work.
- **Loading indicator.** When a network stream is slow to start, or to continue after a seek, the play button shows a spinner. The seek bar shows the parts already received.
- **Shuffle.**
  - Turning shuffle off brings the queue back to its own order: the album's or playlist's, with your additions where you made them.
  - Shuffle on a page turns shuffle on in the player bar.
- **No more freezes under heavy disk writes.** Saving the interface state, the session and the settings no longer runs on the interface thread. The window stays responsive when another program writes a lot.
- **Fixes.**
  - Hardware outputs show their card's name (BIG KNOB STUDIO rather than `hw:3,0`).
  - The signal path popover fits its text.

## For plugin authors

- Users can install your plugin from a link to its release binary or from your GitHub repository; publish a `.sha256` (or a `SHA256SUMS` in the release) so the download is checked.
- The player shows a loading state and the received parts of a stream: relays should start sending quickly and honour `Range` requests.

> From 0.6.0 or later, *Update now* in Settings → About installs this version, checked against the signed `SHA256SUMS`.

> Plugins are written by third parties and are not reviewed by the ricercar project. They run with your permissions: install only plugins you trust, and check that they respect the terms of the service they use.

**From source:** `cargo build --release` with Rust 1.92 or newer (see the README for per-distro dependencies).
